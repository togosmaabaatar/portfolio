What kind of businesses do you work with?+

Small and medium businesses, local service providers and independent projects. My portfolio includes tourism, construction, education and business operations.

Can my website be in English and Mongolian?+

Yes. I can build bilingual navigation and content. We agree on the content and translations as part of the project.

How do pricing and timing work?+

They depend on the pages, features, content and integrations you need. After discussing the scope, we agree on a project plan before development begins.

Yes. We can review its structure and design, refresh content, add features or work on deployment and maintenance.
